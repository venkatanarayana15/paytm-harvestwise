# 🧠 Paytm AI Copilot: The Growth Partner

## 🎯 Vision
Transform Paytm from a **payment processor** to a **trusted business growth partner** for every merchant.

## 🛠 The 3-Layer AI Copilot

| Layer | Component | Role | Business Value |
|-------|-----------|------|----------------|
| **1. Knowledge** | **Sarvam AI** | Understands language, intent, & business logic | *Expert Consultant* - Deciphers merchant needs |

| **3. Hands** | **n8n** | Executes actions (WhatsApp, UI, Orders) | *Operations Team* - Takes action immediately |

## 💡 How It Works (Interactive Flow)

**Scenario:** Merchant says *"How can I increase sales?"*

1.  **Knowledge (Sarvam):**
    *   Input: "How can I increase sales?"
    *   Action: Parses intent (`SALES_GROWTH`)
    *   Output: `{ intent: "GROWTH_ADVICE" }`

2.  **Brain (Cognee):**
    *   Input: `{ intent: "GROWTH_ADVICE" }`
    *   Action: Queries historical sales data + inventory patterns
    *   Output: `{ weekend_spike: true, rice_demand: 40% }`

3.  **Hands (n8n):**
    *   Input: `{ product: "rice", action: "stock_up", reason: "weekend" }`
    *   Action: Sends WhatsApp recommendation + updates UI dashboard
    *   Result: Merchant sees "Stock 10kg Rice extra" alert
