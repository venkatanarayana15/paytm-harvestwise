## 📊 What It Does (7 Powers)

1. **TRACK** - Real-time monitoring of sales, inventory, credit
2. **ANALYZE** - Find patterns (customer habits, sales trends, margins)
3. **SUGGEST** - Proactive ordering, pricing, bundling recommendations
4. **ADVICE** - Strategic coaching on credit, cash flow, seasonal prep
5. **REMEMBER** - Customer profiles, merchant habits, seasonal history (Cognee)
6. **TRIGGER** - Auto-WhatsApp reminders, supplier orders, payment links (n8n)
7. **INCREASE SALES** - Revenue growth via bundles, recovery, optimization
