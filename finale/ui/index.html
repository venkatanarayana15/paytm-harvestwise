AI Business Partner — Business Model Canvas

Every reply is grounded in this canvas: the copilot is the business partner, not a payment widget.

Key Partners

Paytm (Soundbox, payments) · Sarvam (voice) · Cognee (memory) · n8n (automation) · mandi suppliers

Key Activities

Observe → Ask → Approve → Act → Learn loop · voice transcription · grounded restocking · growth nudges

Value Proposition

Voice-first business partner for non-literate merchants. Prevents stock-outs, grows sales, remembers preferences. Low-latency, local-first.

Customer Relationship

WhatsApp copilot (primary) + dashboard copilot. Voice / text / both preference. Language-aware — Tamil default.

Customer Segments

Kirana & vegetable vendors (Lakshmi persona). Sub-segment: cannot read/write → voice-only.

Channels

WhatsApp (WA-AKG) · Paytm Soundbox morning briefing · this dashboard

Key Resources

Deterministic restock engine · memory.json + Cognee graph · Sarvam saaras:v3 + bulbul:v3

Cost Structure

Sarvam API · WA-AKG host · Cognee cloud. Mitigated by bounded executors, health cache, no LLM in the hot loop.

Revenue Streams

Paytm merchant retention + Soundbox attach · supplier lead fees · growth = more GMV

Cognee Knowledge Graph

Sarvam creates facts (transcripts, intents) → add_text → cognify builds the graph → recall retrieves. Every turn is learned; recall warms a 5-minute cache.
