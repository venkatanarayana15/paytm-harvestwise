# 05 — Pitch Strategy & Judging Playbook

## Narrative arc (deck + 3-min pitch)
Hook (Lakshmi story) -> Problem (waste = money in the dustbin) -> Solution (loop: Observe-Predict-Act-Learn) -> Proof (partner stack, defensible math) -> Paytm win (GMV, subscriptions, lending) -> Close ("Every morning, India's merchants open shop. From tomorrow, they open shop with a copilot.")

## The story (open Round-2 pitches with this)
"Lakshmi runs a vegetable cart in Basavanagudi, Bengaluru. She's up at 4 AM at Russell Market, spends Rs 3,000 on stock, and then makes a bet: how much will sell today? Rain, a temple festival, a payday cycle — nobody tells her. At 8 PM she discounts what's left; what doesn't sell is tomorrow's loss. She earns on Paytm. She grows on guesswork. We're ending that."

## Unit-economics model (label as illustrative; footnote assumptions)
- Stock Rs 3,000/day x 26 days = Rs 78,000/mo cost basis; gross margin ~20% -> base profit ~Rs 15,600/mo
- Baseline waste 15% of stock unsold at close, ~40% salvaged via evening discounts -> net loss ~Rs 7,000/mo
- HarvestWise: waste halves (predict + reorder sizing) -> +Rs 3,500/mo; demand-fit stocking (weather/festival/payday) +8-10% sales on ~20% margin -> +Rs 3,000/mo; recovered hours ~5/wk
- Net uplift ~Rs 6,500-8,000/mo ~= +40-50% take-home (waste leverage at 20% margins) — bold but mechanism-sound; pilot will validate
- Paytm-level: at 1M merchants -> Rs 8,000 Cr+/yr merchant earnings unlocked (directional); attach: device subs, GMV per merchant +8%, lending underwriting signals

## Judge Q&A prep
Q: Data privacy? A: Consent-first inside Paytm rails; DPDP-aligned; federated learning shares patterns, never raw data; human approves every money action (n8n gates).
Q: Why not an app? A: Behavior: vendors live on WhatsApp + Paytm already; zero new login; voice = literacy-proof. Distribution: Paytm app/Soundbox base = zero-CAC channel.
Q: Why vegetable vendors — too small? A: Wedge strategy: perishables = highest pain, most predictable signal (weather/calendar/mandi). Same loop generalizes to 30M merchants.
Q: What if Paytm builds it themselves? A: That's the point — Round 1 is a hiring/incubation funnel too. Our moat: per-merchant memory graph + closed-loop vernacular UX; our ask: pilot.
Q: Cold start with no history? A: Cognee federated patterns from similar stalls (category, location, season) bootstrap recommendations on day one.
Q: Why will merchants trust AI advice? A: Every tip is explained in their words ("rain tomorrow -> tomatoes spoil"), one-tap undo, and the weekly scorecard shows money saved. Trust = receipts.
